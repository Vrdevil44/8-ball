## Main post

Been working on something fun: an 8-ball pool coach that runs entirely in your browser.

Real physics engine, ghost-ball aiming lines, and an AI that scores every possible shot and shows you the best one — with the full trajectory drawn on the table. Tap any ball and any pocket and it draws you the exact line.

It's phone-first and works offline once loaded. No install, no account.

Try it: https://vrdevil44.github.io/8-ball/
